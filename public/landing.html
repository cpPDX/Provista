How Provista helps

From “What’s for dinner?”
to “We’ve got everything.”

Provista connects the everyday decisions that happen before, during, and after a grocery trip. It gives the household one clear view without turning groceries into an inventory-management job.

01
□

Plan meals without starting over

Build a weekly plan, save household favorites, repeat meals, account for leftovers, and add what each meal needs.

02
✓

Shop from one shared list

Everyone can add what they need. Organize each store stop into familiar sections, scan barcodes, and check things off quickly while you shop.

03
◫

Know what you have at home

Keep pantry tracking simple with Have, Running low, and Out. Use exact quantities only when they’re genuinely helpful.

04
↗

Understand grocery spending

Keep completed trips, household-paid prices, and spending history together so patterns are easier to see over time.
