How Provista helps

From “What’s for dinner?”
to “We’ve got everything.”

Provista connects the everyday decisions that happen before, during, and after a grocery trip. It gives the household one clear view without turning groceries into an inventory-management job.

01
□

Plan meals without starting over

Your plan and Pantry stay connected. See what upcoming meals are expected to use and what’s missing. Reuse favorites, plan leftovers, and make room for different household planning groups.

02
✓

Tap it and keep moving

Everyone can add to one shared grocery list. Shop by store section, check items off immediately, and use Finish Shopping when you’re done. Buy extra for backup and leave price details for later.

03
◫

Know what you need before you run out

Use Have, Running low, or Out for everyday staples, and Exact tracking when counts help. Account for planned meals before you shop, then correct what you have when real life changes the plan.

04
↗

Add a whole thought at once

Type or paste “milk, bananas, 2 cans black beans, cereal” and review the groceries together. Provista remembers household shorthand after you confirm a match. Scan a barcode instead of typing in List or Pantry.
